Payment, revisions and usage terms
  • Monthly plans are paid 100% in advance for each month.
  • E-commerce and campaign bookings: a 50% advance confirms the booking, and the balance is due before final high-resolution files are released.
  • GST and approved production expenses are additional.
  • A revision round is one consolidated list of reasonable changes from your single point of contact. New concepts and reshoots are quoted separately.
  • After full payment you may use the final images and reels for your own marketing, website, e-commerce listings, and organic and paid social, worldwide with no end date.
  • Campaign shoots include the original raw photo files and camera video clips from the shoot, supplied as captured. Edit timelines, working project files, fonts, stock and licensed music are not included.
  • I may show the work in my portfolio and promotion. Unreleased work is shown only after your launch or with your written approval.
  • Content creation doesn't include posting, account management or ad spend. A signed quote or written approval overrides this summary.
